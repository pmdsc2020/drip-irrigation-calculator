Drip irrigation design calculator

Layout is counted from the grid (laterals × plants per lateral), not from area divided by plant area. Water dose uses crop ET, wetted fraction, efficiency, and irrigation interval. Runtime is the time to apply that dose at the emitter discharge.

System inputs

Use 1.0 for closely spaced vegetables. Orchards are often 0.3–0.7. Set to 1 to match a full-area dose.

If the field is split into equal sets irrigated one after another, pump flow is system flow divided by shifts. Runtime shown is per shift.
